## FDA Regulatory Framework

### 1. FDA Organization

| Center | Responsibility |
|--------|----------------|
| **CDER** | Center for Drug Evaluation and Research |
| **CBER** | Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research |
| **CDRH** | Center for Devices and Radiological Health |
| **ORA** | Office of Regulatory Affairs |
| **OC** | Office of Compliance |

### 2. FDA Inspection Process

| Phase | Activity | Timeline |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Pre-Inspection** | Notice of inspection, preparation | 1-2 weeks |
| **Opening Meeting** | Introduction, scope, logistics | Day 1 |
| **Inspection** | Facility walk-through, document review | 1-5 days |
| **Closing Meeting** | Observations, next steps | Last day |
| **Post-Inspection** | 483 response, corrective actions | 15-30 days |

### 3. FDA Enforcement Actions

| Action | Description | Severity |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Form 483** | Observations of non-compliance | Warning |
| **Warning Letter** | Formal notice of violations | Serious |
| **Import Alert** | Detention without physical examination | Severe |
| **Consent Decree** | Court-ordered corrective actions | Severe |
| **Injunction** | Court order to stop operations | Severe |
| **Seizure** | Product seizure | Severe |
| **Criminal Prosecution** | Individual accountability | Most Severe |

### 4. FDA 483 Response Template

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **1. Observation Summary** | List all observations |
| **2. Root Cause Analysis** | For each observation |
| **3. Corrective Actions** | Immediate and long-term |
| **4. Timeline** | Implementation dates |
| **5. Effectiveness Plan** | Verification method |
