### 2. Audit Types and Frequency

| Audit Type | Frequency | Scope |
|------------|-----------|-------|
| **Internal GMP Audit** | Annual | All GMP areas |
| **Supplier Audit** | Annual/As needed | Critical suppliers |
| **Self-Inspection** | Quarterly | Targeted areas |

## Auditor Qualification

### 1. Auditor Training Requirements

| Training | Hours | Frequency |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| **GMP Fundamentals** | 16 | Initial |
| **Audit Techniques** | 24 | Initial |
| **Auditor Skills** | 16 | Initial |
| **Lead Auditor** | 40 | Initial |
| **Continuing Education** | 16 | Annual |
| **Auditor Feedback** | 8 | Annual |

### 2. Auditor Competency Assessment

| Competency | Assessment Method | Pass Criteria |
|------------|-------------------|---------------|
| **GMP Knowledge** | Written exam | ≥80% |
| **Audit Skills** | Practical assessment | Competent |
| **Report Writing** | Report review | Satisfactory |
| **Communication** | Role play | Satisfactory |
| **Ethics** | Case study | Satisfactory |

### 3. Auditor Qualification Levels

| Level | Requirements | Responsibilities |
|-------|--------------|------------------|
| **Auditor** | Training, 2 audits | Participate in audits |

| **Senior Auditor** | Training, 10 audits, lead 5 | Mentor auditors |
| **Audit Manager** | Training, 15 audits, lead 10 | Manage audit program |
