## Supplier Monitoring

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Formula | Target |
|-----|---------|--------|
| **On-Time Delivery** | (On-time orders / Total orders) × 100 | ≥95% |
| **Quality Acceptance Rate** | (Accepted lots / Total lots) × 100 | ≥98% |
| **Corrective Action Rate** | (CAPAs / Total issues) × 100 | Track |
| **Documentation Accuracy** | (Correct docs / Total docs) × 100 | ≥99% |
| **Response Time** | Average days to respond | <5 days |
