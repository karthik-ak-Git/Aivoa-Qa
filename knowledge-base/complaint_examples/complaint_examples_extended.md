## Case Studies 10-18

### Case 10: Critical - Product Substitution (Cross-Contamination)

**Complaint ID:** COMP-2024-0010
**Product:** Lisinopril 10mg Tablets
**Batch:** LIS20240401A
**Date:** 2024-04-15

**Complaint Text:**
"I found a white oblong tablet in my Lisinopril bottle that looks different from the others. It has no imprint and is larger than my usual tablets."

**Investigation:**
- Visual inspection confirmed one foreign tablet
- Tablet identified as Amlodipine 5mg through visual comparison and dissolution testing
- Root cause: Shared equipment line not properly cleaned between product changeover
- Cross-contamination from previous Amlodipine production run

**Root Cause:** Equipment cleaning failure (Man, Method)
**CAPA:** CAPA-2024-0035
**Outcome:** Recalled batch, enhanced cleaning validation
**Confidence:** 0.95

### Case 11: Major - Packaging Integrity Failure

**Complaint ID:** COMP-2024-0011
**Product:** Metformin 500mg Tablets
**Batch:** MET20240501A
**Date:** 2024-05-20

**Complaint Text:**
"Several tablets in my bottle have turned yellow and have a strange chemical smell. The bottle was stored in my medicine cabinet."

**Investigation:**
- Color change from white to yellow observed
- Chemical odor detected

- Stability testing showed degradation under high humidity conditions

**Root Cause:** Packaging material defect (Material, Measurement)

**Confidence:** 0.94

### Case 13: Critical - Particulate Matter

### Case 14: Major - Device Malfunction

**Complaint ID:** COMP-2024-0014
**Product:** Albuterol MDI
**Batch:** ALB20240801A
**Date:** 2024-08-15

**Complaint Text:**
"My inhaler stopped working after 50 puffs. I shake it and I can still hear liquid inside, but nothing comes out when I press the canister."

**Investigation:**
- Metering valve malfunction confirmed
- Valve stem stuck in closed position
- Residual product confirmed in canister (approximately 80 puffs remaining)
- Root cause: Valve assembly defect in manufacturing

**Root Cause:** Equipment malfunction (Machine, Method)
**CAPA:** CAPA-2024-0062
**Outcome:** Valve supplier qualification, 100% valve testing
**Confidence:** 0.94

### Case 16: Major - Incorrect Strength

### Case 18: Critical - Dosing Device Error

**Complaint ID:** COMP-2024-0018
**Product:** Acetaminophen 160mg/5mL Suspension
**Batch:** ACE20241201A
**Date:** 2024-12-15

**Complaint Text:**
"The oral syringe that came with my child's medication has markings that don't match the dosing chart on the bottle. I gave 1mL but the chart says 1.25mL for my child's weight."

- Potential for dosing errors

**Outcome:** Syringe recall, enhanced kit verification
