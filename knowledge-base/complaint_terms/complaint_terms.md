## 1. Complaint Category Taxonomy (Hierarchical)

### Level 1: Primary Categories

| Category Code | Category Name | Definition | Regulatory Criticality |
|--------------|---------------|------------|----------------------|
| **PC** | **Product Quality Complaint** | Defect in identity, strength, quality, purity of drug product | **Critical** - Direct patient impact |
| **PK** | **Packaging/Labeling Complaint** | Defect in container, closure, label, carton, leaflet | **Major** - May affect identification, stability, safety |
| **LG** | **Logistics/Distribution Complaint** | Issue during storage, transport, delivery | **Major** - May compromise product quality |
| **AD** | **Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR)** | Noxious/unintended response at normal dose | **Critical** - Pharmacovigilance obligation |
| **ME** | **Medication Error** | Preventable event causing inappropriate medication use | **Critical** - Patient safety |
| **RF** | **Regulatory/Filing Inquiry** | Questions about approvals, certificates, specifications | **Minor** - Administrative |
| **OT** | **Other/General Inquiry** | Pricing, availability, samples, technical support | **Minor** - Non-quality |

## 2. Product Quality Subcategories (PC)

| Sub-Code | Subcategory | Definition | Typical Examples |
|----------|-------------|------------|------------------|
| **PC-01** | **Physical Defect - Solid Oral** | Tablet/capsule physical integrity failure | Chipped, cracked, broken, crushed, empty, split, twin, sticky, picking, lamination, capping |
| **PC-02** | **Physical Defect - Liquid/Sterile** | Liquid/sterile product physical anomaly | Particulates, precipitation, discoloration, gas bubbles, cloudiness, incorrect fill volume |
| **PC-03** | **Physical Defect - Semi-solid** | Cream/ointment/gel physical anomaly | Phase separation, grittiness, syneresis, color variation, odor, foreign matter |
| **PC-04** | **Assay/Content Uniformity** | Active ingredient content outside specification | Sub-potent, super-potent, content uniformity failure, degradation products >spec |
| **PC-05** | **Impurity/Contamination** | Unwanted chemical/biological substance | Related substances >spec, residual solvents, elemental impurities, nitrosamines, cross-contamination |
| **PC-06** | **Microbiological** | Microbial quality failure | Sterility failure, TAMC/TYMC >spec, specified organisms, endotoxin, mycoplasma |
| **PC-07** | **Dissolution/Disintegration** | Drug release failure | Dissolution profile failure, disintegration time >spec, dose dumping (ER) |
| **PC-08** | **Stability/Degradation** | Premature quality loss within shelf-life | Out-of-spec at expiry, degradation products, polymorphic conversion, loss of potency |
| **PC-09** | **Foreign Matter** | Extraneous material in/on product | Glass, metal, plastic, fiber, hair, insect parts, machining debris |
| **PC-10** | **Wrong Product/Strength** | Incorrect API, strength, or dosage form | Wrong drug in container, wrong strength labeled, wrong dosage form |

## 6. Complaint Severity Classification Matrix

| Severity Level | Definition | Patient Impact | Regulatory Action | Response Timeline |
|----------------|------------|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Critical (S1)** | Life-threatening, death, permanent disability, congenital anomaly, hospitalization, medically significant | Direct serious harm | **Field Alert Report (FAR) 3 days**, Recall consideration, 15-day ADR | **24 hours** acknowledgment, **5 days** interim |
| **Major (S2)** | Temporary/medically significant harm, product unusable, significant quality system failure | Significant harm possible | Potential Recall (Class I/II), FAR if safety | **5 business days** |
| **Minor (S3)** | Minor inconvenience, no medical intervention, cosmetic defect, administrative error | Negligible harm | Routine processing, trending | **15 business days** |
| **Inquiry (S4)** | Information request, no product defect alleged | None | Routine response | **30 business days** |

### Severity Assignment Rules

| If complaint involves... | Default Severity | Escalation Criteria |
|-------------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| Death/SAE/ADR | Critical (S1) | Any fatality = S1 |
| Sterility failure (injectable/ophthalmic) | Critical (S1) | Any confirmed sterility failure |
| Potency <90% or >110% (narrow TI) | Critical (S1) | Narrow therapeutic index drugs |
| Wrong drug/strength dispensed | Critical (S1) | Medication error potential |
| Sterility failure (oral/topical) | Major (S2) | Unless immunocompromised population |
| Potency OOS (wide TI) | Major (S2) | Unless safety margin narrow |
| Particulates in injectable | Major (S2) | Visible = Critical if glass/metal |
| Dissolution failure (ER) | Major (S2) | Dose dumping risk |
| Label mix-up (wrong strength) | Critical (S1) | Medication error risk |
| Label mix-up (non-safety info) | Minor (S3) | Administrative |
| Cosmetic defect (no function impact) | Minor (S3) | No performance impact |
| Packaging cosmetic (no function) | Minor (S3) | No integrity compromise |

## 8. Investigation Outcome Terminology

| Outcome Code | Term | Definition | Regulatory Implication |
|--------------|------|------------|------------------------|
| **CONF** | **Confirmed** | Defect verified, root cause identified | Full CAPA, regulatory reporting if applicable |
| **UNCONF** | **Unconfirmed** | Insufficient evidence, sample not available, cannot reproduce | Trend monitoring, no CAPA unless trend |
| **USER** | **User Error** | Defect caused by improper handling/storage/use by complainant | Education, labeling review, no product CAPA |
| **COUNTER** | **Counterfeit** | Product confirmed not manufactured by company | Legal, security, supply chain notification |
| **EXPECTED** | **Expected/Inherent** | Known characteristic, documented in labeling (e.g., precipitation on standing) | Labeling adequacy review, no CAPA |
| **TRANSIT** | **Transit Damage** | Damage occurred after release from manufacturer's control | Carrier claim, packaging adequacy review |
| **DUPLICATE** | **Duplicate Complaint** | Same event reported multiple times | Link to original, no new investigation |
| **INQUIRY** | **Information Request** | No defect alleged, information only | Respond, document, no investigation |

## 11. A-Z Complaint Terms Glossary

*120+ standardized terms with pharmaceutical meaning, customer language, and QA terminology mapping.*

---

### A

#### Aberrant
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Deviating from normal or expected product performance
- **Customer Language**: "It's not working right"
- **QA Language**: Product performance deviation
- **Synonyms**: Abnormal, atypical, unusual

#### Abraded
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Surface wear or scraping of a dosage form
- **Customer Language**: "The tablets look scratched"
- **QA Language**: Surface abrasion defect
- **Common Products**: Tablets (film-coated), soft gels

#### Adhesion Failure
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Loss of adhesion between layers or to surface
- **Customer Language**: "The patch won't stick"
- **QA Language**: Adhesion failure, tack loss, edge lift
- **Common Products**: Transdermal patches, labels

#### Agglomerated
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Particles clumped together
- **Customer Language**: "It's all clumpy"
- **QA Language**: Agglomeration
- **Common Products**: Powders, granules

#### Amorphous
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Non-crystalline (solid lacking ordered structure)
- **Customer Language**: "It looks different than before"
- **QA Language**: Amorphous content increase
- **Relevance**: Stability indicator for many APIs

---

### B

#### Bitter Taste
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Undesirable bitter organoleptic property
- **Customer Language**: "It tastes terrible"
- **QA Language**: Taste masking failure
- **Common Products**: Oral liquids, chewable tablets

#### Blistering
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Formation of bubbles on coated surface
- **Customer Language**: "The coating is bubbling"
- **QA Language**: Film blistering
- **Common Products**: Coated tablets

#### Blooming
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Migration of ingredients to surface (appears as white coating)
- **Customer Language**: "White spots on the chocolate/strips"
- **QA Language**: Blooming
- **Common Products**: Chewables, transdermals

#### Bottleneck / Crimp
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Defect in closure crimping
- **Customer Language**: "The cap is loose / won't close"
- **QA Language**: Improper crimping, defective closure

#### Brittle
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Hard but easily broken
- **Customer Language**: "They break so easily"
- **QA Language**: Excessive hardness/friability
- **Common Products**: Tablets, hard capsules

#### Broken
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Dosage form fractured into two or more pieces
- **Customer Language**: "The pills are cracked in half"
- **QA Language**: Broken tablet/capsule/ampoule
- **Severity**: Moderate to High

#### Bubbling
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Gas entrapment in liquid or semisolid
- **Customer Language**: "It's got bubbles in it"
- **QA Language**: Air entrapment, bubble formation

#### Bulging
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Outward swelling of packaging
- **Customer Language**: "The bottle is swollen"
- **QA Language**: Bulging container (potential gas formation)

### C

#### Caking
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Formation of hard mass in powder or granules
- **Customer Language**: "It's turned into a brick"
- **QA Language**: Caking, compaction
- **Common Products**: Powders, granules, effervescent products

#### Capping
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Top or bottom of tablet separates (during manufacturing or handling)
- **Customer Language**: "The pill top is falling off"
- **QA Language**: Capping defect
- **Common Products**: Tablets
- **Root Cause**: Compression issues, air entrapment, poor formulation

#### Chalking
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Surface powder or crumbling on tablet
- **Customer Language**: "It's leaving white powder everywhere"
- **QA Language**: Chalking, friability

#### Chipping
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Small pieces break from tablet edge
- **Customer Language**: "The edges are chipped"
- **QA Language**: Edge chipping
- **Common Products**: Tablets (especially unscored)

#### Cloudiness
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Loss of clarity in solution
- **Customer Language**: "It's cloudy"
- **QA Language**: Solution clarity failure
- **Common Products**: Injections, eye drops, oral solutions

#### Clumping
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Aggregation of particles
- **Customer Language**: "It's clumpy"
- **QA Language**: Agglomeration

#### Coating Defect
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Various defects in coated dosage forms
- **Customer Language**: "The coating is coming off"
- **QA Language**: Coating defect (chipping, peeling, bridging, etc.)
- **Common Products**: Coated tablets

#### Color Change
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Change from specified color
- **Customer Language**: "It used to be white, now it's yellow"
- **QA Language**: Discoloration, color degradation
- **Significance**: Often indicates degradation

#### Contamination (Cross-)
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Presence of unintended material from another product
- **Customer Language**: "I found something else in the bottle"
- **QA Language**: Cross-contamination, foreign matter
- **Severity**: Critical

#### Cracked
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Surface or through-body cracks
- **Customer Language**: "It's got cracks"
- **QA Language**: Cracking
- **Common Products**: Tablets, capsules, suppositories, containers

#### Crumbling
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Disintegrating into small pieces
- **Customer Language**: "It falls apart when I touch it"
- **QA Language**: Friability issue, binding failure

#### Crystallization
- **Pharmaceutical Meaning**: Crystal formation in solution/semisolid
- **Customer Language**: "There are crystals in it"
- **QA Language**: Crystal growth, recrystallization
- **Common Products**: Injections, syrups, gels, creams
