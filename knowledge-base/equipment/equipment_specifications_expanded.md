## 6. Equipment Maintenance Standards

### 6.1 Preventive Maintenance Schedule

| Equipment Type | PM Frequency | PM Activities |
|----------------|--------------|---------------|
| **Granulator** | Weekly | Cleaning, lubrication, blade inspection |
| **Blender** | Weekly | Cleaning, seal inspection, motor check |
| **Compressor** | Weekly | Cleaning, oil change, filter replacement |
| **Coating Pan** | Weekly | Cleaning, spray gun inspection |
| **Packaging Line** | Daily | Cleaning, sensor calibration, belt tension |
| **HPLC** | Monthly | Column inspection, pump seal, detector lamp |
| **HVAC** | Monthly | Filter replacement, belt tension, bearing lubrication |
| **Water System** | Monthly | Sanitization, sample testing, system integrity |

### 6.2 Breakdown Maintenance Response

| Priority | Response Time | Resolution Time | Examples |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|----------|
| **Critical** | < 1 hour | < 4 hours | Product contact equipment failure |
| **High** | < 4 hours | < 24 hours | Packaging line stoppage |
| **Medium** | < 8 hours | < 72 hours | Utility equipment degradation |
| **Low** | < 24 hours | < 1 week | Non-critical support equipment |

## 7. Equipment Failure Modes

### 7.1 Common Failure Modes

| Equipment Type | Failure Mode | Impact | Prevention |
|----------------|--------------|--------|------------|
| **Granulator** | Blade wear | Poor granulation quality | Regular blade inspection |
| **Blender** | Seal failure | Contamination risk | Regular seal replacement |
| **Compressor** | Oil degradation | Air quality failure | Regular oil changes |
| **HPLC** | Column degradation | Assay failure | Column monitoring |
| **Packaging** | Sensor failure | Incorrect count/dose | Regular calibration |
| **Water System** | Biofilm formation | Microbial contamination | Regular sanitization |
| **HVAC** | Filter clogging | Environmental excursion | Regular filter changes |
