## Packaging Defects

### 1. Blister Defects

| Defect | Cause | Prevention |
|--------|-------|------------|
| **Incomplete seal** | Low temperature, pressure | Optimize parameters |
| **Forming defects** | Temperature variation | Control temperature |
| **Print misalignment** | Mechanical issue | Regular maintenance |
| **Component damage** | Handling | Proper training |

### 2. Bottle Defects

| Defect | Cause | Prevention |
|--------|-------|------------|
| **Count error** | Equipment malfunction | Regular calibration |
| **Capping defect** | Torque variation | Optimize torque |
| **Label misalignment** | Equipment issue | Regular adjustment |
| **Coding error** | Operator error | Proper training |

### 3. Injectable Defects

| Defect | Cause | Prevention |
|--------|-------|------------|
| **Particulates** | Environmental | Enhanced monitoring |
| **Fill volume** | Equipment | Calibration |

## Packaging Integrity

### 1. Container Closure Integrity

| Method | Application | Sensitivity |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Vacuum decay** | Semi-rigid containers | High |
| **High-voltage leak** | Glass containers | High |
| **Blue dye** | All containers | Medium |
| **Helium leak** | All containers | Very high |
| **Bubble emission** | All containers | Medium |

### 2. Seal Integrity Testing

| Test | Procedure | Acceptance |
|------|-----------|------------|
| **Peel test** | Measure peel strength | ≥1.5 N/15mm |
| **Burst test** | Apply pressure | No failure |
| **Creep test** | Apply constant pressure | No delamination |

### 3. Package Integrity During Transit

| Test | Standard | Conditions |
|------|----------|------------|
| **ISTA 1A** | Non-synchronized | Drop, vibration |
| **ASTM D4169** | Distribution cycle | Stress, vibration |
| **ASTM D4728** | Vibration testing | Random vibration |
