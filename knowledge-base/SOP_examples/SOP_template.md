## SOP 1: Product Complaint Handling

**Title**: Product Complaint Handling Procedure

### 1. PURPOSE
To establish a system for receiving, documenting, evaluating, investigating, and responding to product complaints in compliance with 21 CFR 211.198.

### 2. SCOPE
All product complaints received from customers, healthcare professionals, distributors, or regulatory authorities regarding finished drug products manufactured or distributed by [Company Name].

### 3. DEFINITIONS
- **Complaint**: Any communication alleging product quality deficiencies
- **Adverse Event**: Adverse medical event associated with product use
- **Serious Adverse Event**: Results in death, hospitalization, disability, etc.
- **Medical Query**: Non-quality related medical information request
- **Complaint Investigator**: Qualified individual assigned to investigate

### 4. RESPONSIBILITIES
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Customer Service | Initial complaint receipt and acknowledgment |
| Quality Assurance | Complaint evaluation, investigation oversight, closure |
| Quality Control | Sample testing as needed |
| Regulatory Affairs | Adverse event reporting |
| Manufacturing | Technical investigation support |

### 5. PROCEDURE

#### 5.1 Complaint Receipt
1. All complaints received by any department must be forwarded to QA within 1 business day
2. QA assigns a unique complaint number (CMP-YYYY-NNNN)
3. Initial information recorded in complaint management system:
   - Date received
   - Complainant name and contact information
   - Product name and strength
   - Lot number (if available)
   - Nature of complaint (customer's words)
   - Quantity involved
   - Channel (phone, email, web, letter)

#### 5.2 Initial Evaluation
1. QA evaluates within 24 hours:
   - Is this a complaint or an adverse event?
   - Is the complaint related to product quality?
   - Is immediate action required?
   - What is the severity level?

#### 5.3 Severity Classification
| Level | Criteria | Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Patient safety risk, sterility failure, wrong product | 24 hours |
| Major | Potential quality impact, potency issue, labeling error | 48 hours |
| Moderate | Physical defect, packaging issue, taste/odor | 5 business days |
| Minor | Cosmetic issue, customer service issue | 10 business days |

#### 5.4 Investigation
1. QA assigns investigator based on complaint type
2. Investigator collects:
   - Batch production record
   - Quality control test results
   - Retained sample (if available)
   - Product photographs (if available)
   - Equipment and environmental data
3. Root cause analysis performed
4. Impact assessment:
   - Other batches affected?
   - Product recall needed?
   - Regulatory reporting needed?
5. Investigation documented in complaint report

#### 5.5 Customer Response
1. QA prepares response letter
2. Response includes:
   - Acknowledgment of complaint
   - Investigation summary (as appropriate)
   - Corrective actions taken (as appropriate)
   - Replacement/refund (if applicable)
3. Response sent within investigation timeline

#### 5.6 CAPA Initiation
1. If root cause identified, CAPA initiated per SOP-CAPA-001
2. CAPA tracked separately and linked to complaint record

#### 5.7 Regulatory Reporting
1. Adverse events reported per SOP-REG-001
2. Serious adverse events within 15 calendar days
3. Product defects with potential health risk reported to applicable authorities

#### 5.8 Closure
1. Investigation complete
2. Customer response sent
3. CAPA (if any) initiated
4. All documents filed
5. QA approves closure

### 6. REFERENCES
- 21 CFR 211.198: Complaint Files
- 21 CFR 314.80: Postmarketing Adverse Event Reporting
- ICH Q10: Pharmaceutical Quality System
- SOP-CAPA-001: Corrective and Preventive Action
- SOP-REG-001: Regulatory Reporting

### 7. ATTACHMENTS
- Form F-001: Complaint Intake Form
- Form F-002: Complaint Investigation Report

## SOP 2: Investigation of Deviations

**Title**: Deviation Investigation Procedure

### 1. PURPOSE
To define the process for identifying, documenting, investigating, and closing deviations in accordance with cGMP requirements.

### 2. SCOPE
All deviations from approved procedures, specifications, or standards that occur during manufacturing, packaging, testing, storage, or distribution.

### 3. PROCEDURE HIGHLIGHTS

#### 3.1 Immediate Actions
1. Any person discovering a deviation must:
   a. Stop affected activities if product quality may be impacted
   b. Notify supervisor immediately
   c. Quarantine affected material
   d. Document in deviation log

#### 3.2 Documentation
1. Person discovering deviation completes initial deviation form:
   - Date and time of deviation
   - Description of what occurred
   - Product and batch number
   - Operation/step where deviation occurred
   - Immediate actions taken
   - Signatures

#### 3.3 Investigation
1. QA assigns deviation number (DEV-YYYY-NNNN)
2. Classify as Critical, Major, or Minor
3. Investigation within 5 working days
4. Root cause analysis using appropriate tools
5. Product disposition decision (release / rework / reject)

#### 3.4 Closure
1. All actions completed
2. Effectiveness verified
3. QA approved

## SOP 3: CAPA Procedure

**Title**: Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) Procedure

### 1. PURPOSE
To establish a systematic process for implementing corrective and preventive actions in response to quality issues.

### 2. SCOPE
All CAPA activities triggered by complaints, deviations, OOS results, audits, or quality metrics.

### 3. PROCEDURE HIGHLIGHTS

#### 3.1 CAPA Initiation
1. Trigger identified (complaint, deviation, OOS, audit finding, trend)
2. CAPA form initiated with reference to source document
3. CAPA number assigned (CAPA-YYYY-NNNN)

#### 3.2 Root Cause Analysis
1. Cross-functional team assembled
2. Appropriate RCA tool selected
3. True root cause(s) identified

#### 3.3 Action Plan
1. Define specific actions
2. Assign owners and due dates
3. Include both corrective and preventive actions

#### 3.4 Effectiveness Check
1. Define measurable acceptance criteria
2. Verify after implementation
3. Include monitoring period

#### 3.5 Closure
1. All actions completed
2. Effectiveness confirmed
3. QA approval

## SOP 4: OOS Investigation

**Title**: Out-of-Specification (OOS) Test Results Investigation

### 1. PURPOSE
To establish a procedure for investigating OOS test results in compliance with FDA OOS Guidance (2006).

### 2. SCOPE
All laboratory test results that fall outside established specifications.

### 3. KEY STEPS

#### Phase I: Laboratory Investigation
1. Analyst preserves all data and materials
2. Supervisory review of calculations, technique, equipment
3. If lab error found: invalidate OOS, document, re-test
4. If no lab error: proceed to Phase II

#### Phase II: Full Investigation
1. Manufacturing investigation
2. Additional testing (retains, re-sampling)
3. Root cause analysis
4. CAPA implementation

## SOP 5: Returned Goods Handling

**Title**: Handling of Returned Drug Products

### 1. PURPOSE
To define procedures for receiving, inspecting, evaluating, and disposing of returned drug products.

### 2. KEY POINTS
- All returned products received in quarantine
- Visual inspection for tampering, damage, condition
- Temperature excursion evaluation
- Products stored at proper conditions within expiry may be returned to stock
- All other returns destroyed or disposed

**Reference**: 21 CFR 211.204
