## SOP-QA-004: Change Control Management

### Document Control

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **SOP Number** | SOP-QA-004 |
| **Title** | Change Control Management |
| **Version** | 1.0 |
| **Effective Date** | 2024-01-15 |
| **Review Date** | 2026-01-15 |
| **Author** | Quality Assurance |
| **Approved By** | QA Director |

### 1. Purpose

To establish a standardized process for managing changes to documents, processes, equipment, materials, and systems that may affect product quality or cGMP compliance.

### 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all changes including:
- Document changes (SOPs, Specifications, Methods)
- Process changes (Manufacturing, Packaging, Labeling)
- Equipment changes (New equipment, Modifications, Replacements)
- Material changes (Suppliers, Specifications, Components)
- System changes (Computerized systems, IT systems)

### 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Change Initiator** | Complete change request form, provide justification |
| **Department Manager** | Review and approve departmental impact |
| **Quality Assurance** | Assess regulatory impact, approve/reject change |
| **Regulatory Affairs** | Assess regulatory filing requirements |
| **Subject Matter Experts** | Provide technical assessment |

### 4. Procedure

#### 4.1 Change Request Initiation
1. Initiator completes Change Request Form (Form-QA-004)
2. Provides description, justification, and proposed implementation date
3. Identifies affected departments and products

#### 4.2 Impact Assessment
1. QA assigns change category (Minor, Major, Critical)
2. Cross-functional team assesses impact
3. Regulatory filing requirements identified
4. Risk assessment conducted for Major/Critical changes

#### 4.3 Review and Approval
1. Department managers review departmental impact
2. QA reviews regulatory impact
3. Regulatory Affairs reviews filing requirements
4. Final approval by QA Director (Major/Critical changes)

#### 4.4 Implementation
1. Change implemented according to approved plan
2. Training conducted as required
3. Documentation updated
4. Effectiveness verified

#### 4.5 Change Closure
1. All actions completed and verified
2. Change record closed by QA
3. Related documents updated in document management system

### 5. Change Categories

| Category | Description | Approval Level | Timeline |
|----------|-------------|----------------|----------|
| **Minor** | No regulatory impact, no product quality impact | Department Manager + QA | 30 days |
| **Major** | May require regulatory notification/filing | QA Director + Regulatory | 60 days |
| **Critical** | Requires regulatory approval before implementation | VP Quality + Regulatory | 90 days |

### 6. References

- FDA 21 CFR 211.100
- ICH Q10 Section 3.2
- EU GMP Chapter 1

### 7. Forms

- Form-QA-004: Change Request Form
- Form-QA-004a: Change Impact Assessment
- Form-QA-004b: Change Implementation Checklist

## SOP-QA-005: Audit Management

### Document Control

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **SOP Number** | SOP-QA-005 |
| **Title** | Audit Management |
| **Version** | 1.0 |
| **Effective Date** | 2024-02-01 |
| **Review Date** | 2026-02-01 |
| **Author** | Quality Assurance |
| **Approved By** | QA Director |

### 1. Purpose

To establish a standardized process for planning, conducting, and following up on internal and external audits to ensure compliance with cGMP, regulations, and quality system requirements.

### 2. Scope

This SOP applies to:
- Internal audits (Self-inspections)
- Supplier/vendor audits
- Regulatory inspections
- Customer audits

### 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Audit Program Manager** | Develop audit schedule, assign auditors |
| **Lead Auditor** | Plan and conduct audits |
| **Auditor** | Conduct audit activities |
| **Audit Program Manager** | Review audit findings |
| **Department Manager** | Implement corrective actions |

### 4. Procedure

#### 4.1 Audit Planning
1. Develop annual audit schedule
2. Select auditors based on expertise
3. Prepare audit plan and checklist
4. Notify auditee of audit schedule

#### 4.2 Audit Execution
1. Conduct opening meeting
2. Execute audit plan
3. Document findings (observations, minor/major findings)
4. Conduct closing meeting
5. Prepare audit report

#### 4.3 Audit Follow-up
1. Issue corrective action requests
2. Verify corrective actions implemented
3. Close audit findings
4. Update audit database

### 5. Audit Findings Categories

| Category | Description | Response Timeline |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Observation** | Opportunity for improvement | Next audit cycle |
| **Minor Finding** | Isolated non-compliance | 30 days |
| **Major Finding** | Systemic non-compliance | 15 days |
| **Critical Finding** | Patient safety risk | Immediate |

### 6. References

- FDA 21 CFR 211.180
- ICH Q10 Section 4.1
- EU GMP Chapter 1

### 7. Forms

- Form-QA-005: Audit Report
- Form-QA-005a: Audit Checklist

## SOP-QA-006: Training and Competency

### Document Control

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **SOP Number** | SOP-QA-006 |
| **Title** | Training and Competency Management |
| **Version** | 1.0 |
| **Effective Date** | 2024-03-01 |
| **Review Date** | 2026-03-01 |
| **Author** | Training/HR |
| **Approved By** | QA Director |

### 1. Purpose

To establish a standardized process for training personnel and assessing competency to ensure they can perform their assigned tasks competently.

### 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all GMP personnel including:
- Manufacturing personnel
- Quality Control personnel
- Quality Assurance personnel
- Warehouse personnel
- Engineering/Maintenance personnel
- Administrative personnel

### 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Department Manager** | Identify training needs, approve training plans |
| **Trainer** | Develop and deliver training |
| **Trainee** | Complete training, demonstrate competency |
| **Training Coordinator** | Maintain training records |
| **QA** | Verify training compliance |

### 4. Procedure

#### 4.1 Training Needs Assessment
1. Identify training requirements from SOPs, regulations, job descriptions
2. Assess current competency levels
3. Develop individual training plans
4. Prioritize training based on risk

#### 4.2 Training Delivery
1. Conduct initial training for new employees
2. Conduct refresher training as required
3. Conduct specialized training for new procedures/equipment
4. Document all training activities

#### 4.3 Competency Assessment
1. Conduct written assessments for knowledge verification
2. Conduct practical assessments for skills verification
3. Observe on-the-job performance
4. Document competency results

#### 4.4 Training Records
1. Maintain individual training files
2. Track training completion
3. Generate training compliance reports
4. Archive training records per retention policy

### 5. Training Categories

| Category | Frequency | Method |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| **Initial Training** | Upon hire | Classroom + OJT |
| **Refresher Training** | Annual | Classroom or online |
| **SOP Training** | Upon revision | Read & Understand |
| **Equipment Training** | Upon qualification | Hands-on |
| **GMP Training** | Annual | Classroom or online |
| **Safety Training** | Annual | Classroom or online |

### 6. Competency Assessment Methods

| Method | Application | Pass Criteria |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Written Test** | Knowledge assessment | 80% score |
| **Practical Assessment** | Skills assessment | Competent rating |
| **Observation** | On-the-job performance | Satisfactory rating |
| **Interview** | Understanding verification | satisfactory responses |

### 7. References

- FDA 21 CFR 211.25
- ICH Q10 Section 4.2
- EU GMP Chapter 2

### 8. Forms

- Form-QA-006: Training Record
- Form-QA-006a: Competency Assessment
- Form-QA-006b: Training Plan

## SOP-QA-007: Document Control

### Document Control

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **SOP Number** | SOP-QA-007 |
| **Title** | Document Control |
| **Version** | 1.0 |
| **Effective Date** | 2024-04-01 |
| **Review Date** | 2026-04-01 |
| **Author** | Quality Assurance |
| **Approved By** | QA Director |

### 1. Purpose

To establish a standardized process for creating, reviewing, approving, distributing, and controlling quality system documents.

### 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all GMP documents including:
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
- Work Instructions (WIs)
- Specifications
- Methods
- Forms and Templates
- Batch Records
- Validation Documents

### 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Document Author** | Draft document per template |
| **Reviewer** | Review document for accuracy and completeness |
| **Approver** | Approve document for use |
| **Document Control** | Distribute, control, and archive documents |
| **User** | Use only current approved documents |

### 4. Procedure

#### 4.1 Document Creation
1. Use approved document template
2. Follow document numbering convention
3. Include required sections (Purpose, Scope, Responsibilities, Procedure)
4. Attach supporting references and forms

#### 4.2 Document Review
1. Technical review by subject matter expert
2. Quality review by QA
3. Regulatory review if applicable
4. Incorporate comments and finalize

#### 4.3 Document Approval
1. Department manager approval (operational documents)
2. QA approval (quality-related documents)
3. Executive approval (policies)

#### 4.4 Document Distribution
1. Issue controlled copies
2. Withdraw obsolete copies
3. Maintain distribution list
4. Ensure current versions available at point of use

### 5. Document Numbering

| Document Type | Format | Example |
|---------------|--------|---------|
| **SOP** | SOP-[Dept]-[###] | SOP-QA-001 |
| **Work Instruction** | WI-[Dept]-[###] | WI-MFG-001 |
| **Specification** | SPEC-[Product]-[###] | SPEC-LIP-001 |
| **Method** | METH-[Dept]-[###] | METH-QC-001 |
| **Form** | FORM-[Dept]-[###] | FORM-QA-001 |

### 6. References

- ICH Q10 Section 4.2
- EU GMP Chapter 4

## SOP-QA-008: Recall Management

### Document Control

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **SOP Number** | SOP-QA-008 |
| **Title** | Product Recall Management |
| **Version** | 1.0 |
| **Effective Date** | 2024-05-01 |
| **Review Date** | 2026-05-01 |
| **Author** | Quality Assurance |
| **Approved By** | QA Director |

### 1. Purpose

To establish a standardized process for initiating, executing, and completing product recalls to protect patient safety and comply with regulatory requirements.

### 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all product recalls including:
- Voluntary recalls initiated by the company
- FDA-requested recalls
- Mandatory recalls
- Market withdrawals

### 3. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Recall Coordinator** | Manage recall process |
| **QA Director** | Approve recall decision |
| **Regulatory Affairs** | Notify regulatory authorities |
| **Sales/Distribution** | Notify customers, recover product |
| **Manufacturing** | Provide batch records, support investigation |

### 4. Procedure

#### 4.1 Recall Initiation
1. Identify need for recall
2. Conduct risk assessment (Class I, II, III)
3. Obtain approval from QA Director
4. Notify regulatory authorities (FDA, EMA, etc.)

#### 4.2 Recall Execution
1. Issue recall notification to customers
2. Provide product identification information
3. Arrange product return/destruction
4. Track recall effectiveness

#### 4.3 Recall Completion
1. Compile recall effectiveness report
2. Submit to regulatory authorities
3. Document lessons learned
4. Implement corrective actions

### 5. Recall Classes

| Class | Definition | Health Risk | Timeline |
|-------|------------|-------------|----------|
| **Class I** | Reasonable probability of serious adverse health consequences or death | High | Immediate |
| **Class II** | May cause temporary or medically reversible adverse health consequences | Medium | 24 hours |
| **Class III** | Not likely to cause adverse health consequences | Low | 72 hours |

### 6. References

- FDA 21 CFR Part 7
- FDA Guidance: Recalls
- EU GMP Chapter 8

### 7. Forms

- Form-QA-008: Recall Notification
- Form-QA-008a: Recall Effectiveness Report
- Form-QA-008b: Product Return Record
