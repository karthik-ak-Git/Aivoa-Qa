## Part 210: General Provisions

### § 210.1 - Status of cGMP Regulations
- Regulations in Parts 210, 211, 213, 225, 226 contain **minimum** cGMP requirements
- Cover methods, facilities, controls for manufacture, processing, packing, holding
- Ensure drug meets safety, identity, strength, quality, purity
- **Failure to comply** = drug adulterated under FD&C Act §501(a)(2)(B)
- Subject to regulatory action

### § 210.2 - Applicability
- Applies to manufacture, processing, packing, holding of drugs
- Includes: Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), finished dosage forms
- Covers all drugs for US market (prescription, OTC, investigational)

### § 210.3 - Definitions (Key Terms)
| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Batch** | Specific quantity of drug intended to have uniform character/quality, produced according to single manufacturing order |
| **Batch Record** | Complete compilation of documentation for batch |
| **Component** | Any ingredient intended for use in drug product (active/inactive) |
| **Drug Product** | Finished dosage form (tablet, capsule, solution, etc.) containing active ingredient |
| **Lot** | Synonymous with batch |
| **Manufacture/Processing/Packing/Holding** | All operations from receipt of materials through distribution |
| **Quality Control Unit** | Organizational element responsible for quality duties |
| **Representative Sample** | Sample drawn by rational criteria to accurately portray material |
| **Strength** | Concentration of active ingredient per dosage unit/volume |
| **Theoretical Yield** | Quantity expected at any stage based on component quantities |
| **Actual Yield** | Quantity actually produced at any stage |
| **Percentage of Theoretical Yield** | (Actual/Theoretical) × 100 |

## Quality Control Unit (211.22) - Central Authority

### Responsibilities
| Area | QC Unit Authority |
|------|-------------------|
| **Approval/Rejection** | All components, drug products, containers, closures, in-process materials, packaging, labeling |
| **Review** | Production records (master & batch) before release |
| **Investigation** | Errors, deviations, complaints, OOS results |
| **Stability** | Approve stability protocols, evaluate data |
| **Change Control** | Review/approve changes affecting quality |
| **Vendor** | Approve/qualify suppliers |
| **Recall** | Coordinate/manage recalls |

### Independence Requirement
- QC unit separate from production
- Direct access to management
- Adequate resources and authority

## Key Compliance Principles

### 1. Written Procedures (SOPs)
> "There shall be written procedures for..." - Appears 50+ times in Part 211

### 2. Documentation (ALCOA+)
- **Attributable** - Who, when
- **Legible** - Readable
- **Contemporaneous** - Real-time
- **Original** - First capture
- **Accurate** - Error-free
- **Complete** - All data
- **Consistent** - Logical sequence
- **Enduring** - Durable
- **Available** - Accessible

### 3. Data Integrity
- Audit trails (21 CFR Part 11)
- No deletion/modification without trace
- Backup and recovery

### 4. Validation Life Cycle
- **Prospective** - Before commercial distribution
- **Concurrent** - During initial production
- **Retrospective** - Historical data review
- **Revalidation** - After changes

### 5. Change Control
- Written procedures
- Impact assessment (quality, regulatory, validation)
- Approval before implementation
- Documentation
