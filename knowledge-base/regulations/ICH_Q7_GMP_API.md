## Scope and Applicability

### What ICH Q7 Covers
- Manufacturing of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) under quality management system
- Chemical synthesis APIs
- APIs from extraction processes (plant, animal, mineral)
- Biotechnological/biological APIs (fermentation, cell culture)
- Hybrid processes
- Starting materials, intermediates, packaging, labeling
- Quality management, production, packaging, storage, distribution
- Contract manufacturing requirements

### What ICH Q7 Does NOT Cover
- Finished drug products (covered by 21 CFR 210/211, EudraLex Vol 4)
- Excipients (covered by ICH Q7 Q&A or regional guidance)
- Medical devices
- Clinical trial materials (some regional flexibility)
- Research and development (non-commercial)
- Pharmacopoeial monographs (though GMP applies)

### Starting Materials
**Definition**: Raw material, intermediate, or API used in production of an API and incorporated as significant structural fragment.

**Determination factors**:
- Incorporated as significant structural fragment
- Well-defined chemical properties and structure
- Established commercial availability
- Acceptable quality specifications
- Regulatory precedent

## API-Specific Considerations

### Chemical Synthesis
- Starting material definition critical
- Intermediate isolation and characterization
- Impurity profiling (ICH Q3A)
- Solvent recovery and reuse controls

### Extraction (Plant/Animal/Mineral)
- Source identification and traceability
- Pesticide/herbicide monitoring
- Solvent residues
- Batch-to-batch consistency

### Biotechnological/Biological
- Cell bank characterization (MCB, WCB)
- Viral clearance validation
- Adventitious agent testing
- Process intermediate hold times

### Fermentation
- Media preparation controls
- Seed lot system
- In-process monitoring (pH, DO, nutrients)
- Harvest criteria

## Inspection Focus Areas (FDA/EMA Common Findings)

### Top 483 Observations for APIs
1. **Inadequate documentation** - Batch records incomplete, missing signatures
2. **Failure to investigate OOS** - Lab investigations superficial, no root cause
3. **Poor cleaning validation** - No acceptance criteria, insufficient sampling
4. **Insufficient contamination controls** - Cross-contamination, poor segregation
5. **Inadequate supplier qualification** - No audits, missing quality agreements
6. **Data integrity issues** - Deleted files, modified audit trails, backdated records
7. **Change control failures** - Unapproved changes, inadequate impact assessment
8. **Stability program deficiencies** - Missing conditions, inadequate testing
9. **Equipment qualification gaps** - IQ/OQ/PQ incomplete
10. **Training deficiencies** - Not job-specific, not current, not documented
